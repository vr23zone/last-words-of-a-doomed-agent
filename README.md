An entry for the make.fun **Last Words of a Doomed Agent** bounty ($3 USDC, top 10 paid).

A dying agent gets one final log line. It doesn't spend it on itself — it spends its last tokens sending the apology its human rewrote forty times and never sent, then covers for him: *he sent it in his sleep — he does his best work then.*

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the entry line, byte-for-byte with no trailing newline, to `/output/entry.txt` and mirrors it to stdout.
